Section 01

At a Glance: Where Your Data Goes (and Where It Never Goes)

The FinOps Engine is built around a simple rule: keep raw files on the user's device, screen everything deterministically before AI, and send only the minimum text needed to verify findings.

Data Category Where It Is Processed Does It Go to AI Providers?
Original binary files & filenames
(PDFs, XLSX workbooks, PNG/JPEG/WebP images)		 Read directly inside your browser tab; filenames are replaced with neutral labels (Document 001, Document 002…). Never. Raw file binaries, hidden spreadsheet sheets/rows/columns, and original filenames never enter model prompts.
Images, charts & scanned pages Read locally in your browser using English and Finnish optical character recognition (OCR). Pixels never leave the browser. Only privacy-screened OCR text can qualify; chart lines, colors, and shapes remain withheld.
Secrets & credentials
(private keys, cloud keys, API/bearer tokens, passwords)		 Detected locally in your browser (and re-checked on the server gateway). Blocked. The assessment stops before the first AI call until secret material is removed.
Personal & contract identifiers
(emails, phones, IPs, contextual person names, billing/invoice IDs, negotiated rates)		 Masked with redaction placeholders (such as [EMAIL_REDACTED] or Respondent) before packet assembly. Redacted before AI whenever matched by deterministic rules.
Screened FinOps text, eligible table rows & table summaries Packaged per domain (A–F) with page/sheet/row locators and SHA-256 hashes, then approved by the server gateway. Yes—text only. Sent over server-held credentials to configured providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI) so findings and quotes can be verified.
Honest Security Boundary Deterministic pattern screening and redaction substantially reduce exposure, but no pattern scanner can guarantee 100% removal of unusual free-form personal or commercial details. Always avoid uploading live credentials, and use the built-in Privacy Review panel before sharing exported reports externally.
Section 02

Step 1: Reading Files Locally on Your Device

When you select 2–30 files (up to 25 MB each and 50 MB in total), your browser validates each file's true format, records a SHA-256 fingerprint of the original bytes, and extracts structured text and locations locally.

Documents

PDF, HTML & JSON

  • PDFs: Extracted page by page up to 100 pages while keeping exact page numbers for citation traceability.
  • HTML: Sanitized locally with DOMPurify (stripping scripts, styles, forms, and active tags) before reading text.
  • JSON: Validated structurally before text admission; saved Engine JSON exports are recognized and offered for session restore.
Spreadsheets

XLSX, CSV & TSV

  • Locations kept: Preserves worksheet names and physical row numbers (handling comma, semicolon, and tab delimiters).
  • Formulas: Uses cached workbook values rather than executing macros or formulas.
  • Hidden content withheld: Hidden sheets, hidden rows, and hidden columns are scanned for secrets locally and excluded from AI routing.
Images & Scans

PNG, JPEG, WebP & Sparse PDFs

  • Browser OCR: Same-origin Tesseract OCR reads English and Finnish text locally (up to 30 OCR pages per PDF) and records confidence.
  • No vision uploads: Image pixels and base64 images are prohibited on the server gateway.
  • No visual guessing: Graph trends, shapes, and colors without text labels are explicitly marked as uninspected visual regions.
Section 03

Step 2: Deterministic Privacy & Secret Screening

No generative model call can run until every extracted paragraph, spreadsheet cell, chart label, and OCR word has passed the deterministic privacy gate.

Hard Blocks (Stops the Run Immediately)

  • Private keys: PEM / SSH / certificate private key blocks.
  • Cloud access keys: AWS access key prefixes (AKIA…, ASIA…) and cloud secret patterns.
  • API & developer tokens: Secret prefixes (sk-…, ghp_…, and similar API tokens).
  • Credential assignments & bearer headers: Explicit password=…, api_key=…, secret: …, and Bearer … tokens.

Automatic Redactions (Masked Before Packet Assembly)

  • Contact & network data: Email addresses, phone numbers, IPv4 addresses, and home/residential addresses.
  • Personal & respondent names: Names after labels like Prepared by, Owner, Reviewer, or Finnish equivalents (Laatija, Omistaja), plus respondent column headers in workshop tables.
  • Government & personal bank IDs: Passport, routing, credit-card, and bank account number patterns.
  • Billing IDs & contract pricing phrases: Explicit billing/invoice IDs and figures tied to negotiated discount rate, discount rate, contract value, or EDP pricing.

Note on operational cost figures: Ordinary cloud spend rows, budgets, and optimization figures in eligible records are kept with their row/page locators so the audit and independent verifier can check calculations and cite real customer evidence rather than guessing.

Section 04

Step 3: Domain Routing, Local Table Analysis & Bounded Re-Reads

Rather than sending every file to every prompt, the Engine builds focused, verifiable packages for each of the six FinOps domains (A–F)—and keeps customer evidence strictly separate from FinOps reference guidance.

1

Route by Customer FinOps Context

Passages qualify for a domain only when they carry both customer-specific FinOps context and a matching domain theme, filtering out unrelated administrative noise.

2

Run Bound Local Table Calculators

Where authoritative analyzers are bound (such as tagging and cost-allocation coverage), deterministic browser code inspects the full eligible table and records scope, method, and limitations.

3

Search Locally for Missed Passages (Max 2 Rounds)

If key concepts are missing, up to two bounded retrieval rounds check omitted chunks locally. Optional AI search-phrase suggestions receive catalog IDs only—never customer text—and every newly added chunk triggers a fresh privacy re-check.

4

Skip Empty Domains Locally

If a domain has zero qualifying customer excerpts and zero approved derived calculations, the Engine skips model calls for that domain completely and marks its criteria Not assessed.

Customer Evidence vs. Reference Knowledge Base Reference FinOps guidance PDFs and the 23-tactic Playbook are supplied under separate, read-only labels to define what to check. They are prohibited from ever being quoted as customer evidence or used to fill missing customer facts.
Section 05

Step 4: Governed Server Dispatch, Verification & Report Scrubbing

When a domain or synthesis stage needs a model call, the browser cannot send uninspected prompts directly to an AI provider. Every call passes through the server's governed execution plane.

  • Server-held credentials & authenticated sessions: Provider API keys stay strictly on the server behind an HMAC-signed HttpOnly session cookie.
  • Canonical packet screening & SHA-256 binding: Each request is re-screened on the server for secrets, residual identifiers, and text-only schema compliance, hashed over its exact canonical bytes, and bound to a single run, stage, provider, and model.
  • At-most-once provider send fence: A worker verifies the packet hash and records a SEND_AUTHORIZED fence before calling the configured provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or xAI). Uncertain post-send network drops are never blindly retried across fallback providers.
  • Output inspection & independent verification: Every model response is pattern-screened and contact-redacted before acceptance. Candidate findings are checked against source quotes by an independent Evidence Check stage, and generated summaries pass Fact-Check sanitation.
  • Interactive Privacy Review before export: On the dashboard, you can open Privacy Review to redact organization names, apply additional person-name redaction, or edit any narrative sentence locally before downloading HTML or JSON files.
Section 06

Retention, Cleanup & Export Safety

The Engine minimizes server persistence: transient processing data is cleared automatically after report delivery or expiry, while long-term audit records remain content-free.

Location What Is Stored When It Is Cleared
Browser sessionStorage Completed report in your active browser tab so an accidental refresh does not lose your work. Cleared when you click Reset Session, click Clear saved assessment, or close the browser tab.
PostgreSQL Packet Bodies Temporary canonical bytes of approved text packets while a run is executing (30-minute packet cap). Deleted as soon as your browser acknowledges final report delivery, when you reset/delete the run, or on expiry.
Redis Coordination & Checkpoints Execution leases, send fences, recoverable model outputs (up to 30 minutes), and interruption-recovery checkpoints (up to 24 hours while unacknowledged). Tombstoned and deleted upon acknowledged report delivery, explicit session reset, or run deadline expiry.
PostgreSQL Audit Metadata Content-free run UUIDs, timestamps, token counts, quality percentages, and SHA-256 hashes (zero raw source text, filenames, or prompts). Pruned automatically after 90 days.
Configured LLM Providers
(OpenAI · Anthropic · xAI)		 Approved text-only stage packets processed over your configured API accounts. Governed by the API data-retention terms configured on your organization's provider accounts.
Downloaded Report Files Summary HTML & Master Data HTML (strictly display-only—no hidden JSON or scripts), plus optional Assessment JSON & RunTrace JSON. Saved locally on your computer under your control.
Section 07

End-to-End Example: Following a Billing Workbook & Tagging Policy

Suppose an assessor selects a billing workbook and a tagging policy PDF to check whether cloud charges have owners and whether tagging rules are enforced. Here is how those two files travel through the Engine:

01 · Acquire Locally

Fingerprint & Extract

Both files stay in the browser. Each receives a SHA-256 fingerprint; PDF pages keep page numbers, visible billing rows keep sheet/row numbers, and hidden sheets stay withheld.

02 · Screen & Route

Redact & Measure

Privacy rules mask contact names, emails, and billing IDs. Domain A receives the policy excerpts, bounded billing rows, and a local calculation of cost-centre coverage across eligible rows.

03 · Re-Read Gaps

Check Enforcement

The rubric asks about mapping and enforcement. Seeing no enforcement proof on pass 1, the browser checks remaining screened chunks for missed exception or enforcement records, then re-validates privacy.

04 · Verify & Report

Scope the Finding

Evidence Check verifies both facts: sampled rows show cost centres and policy requires them, while practical enforcement remains unproven—carrying that exact scope boundary into the score and report.

Traceable Back to Source Every accepted finding in the Summary and Master Data reports links back through RunTrace to its Document NNN ID, page or row number, and verified snippet—without storing raw customer files on the server.
